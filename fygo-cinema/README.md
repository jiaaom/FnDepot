# Fygo Cinema

English | [简体中文](README.zh-CN.md)

Fygo TV on your TV. The NAS's own Fygo TV web app runs fullscreen on a TV
plugged into the NAS, driven by a remote, with hardware video decoding.

![Home page on the TV, with the focus ring the remote moves](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jiaaom/fygo-cinema/main/assets/home.jpg)

- **Remote navigation** on every page, including the player's controls
  (speed, quality, subtitles, audio track).
- **Signs in by itself** with the account entered on its admin page, and stays
  signed in.
- **Follows the TV:** it starts when the TV is plugged in and plays through the
  TV's speakers. The remote's volume keys set the TV's volume.

Source: <https://github.com/jiaaom/fygo-cinema>

## Install order

1. **Fygo TV** (`trim.media`), from App Center
2. **Appliance Compositor** (`appliance-compositor`)
3. **Fygo Cinema** (`fygo-cinema`)

Then open Fygo Cinema on the fnOS desktop, enter the Fygo TV account the TV
should use, and plug in the TV. Needs an Intel GPU.
